FAQ

Before you order.

Can I order online and pick it up at the store?

Yes. During checkout you can select store pickup where available. The team will confirm when your order is ready.

Do you offer delivery?

Delivery can be arranged depending on your location, the order and availability. Delivery details will be confirmed by the business.

Can I buy materials in bulk?

Yes. Contact us with the materials, specifications and quantities you need for a bulk quotation.

Will every product have a fixed price?

Some products can display a listed price, while others may show “Price on request” because availability and market prices can change.

Can I simply visit the physical store?

Yes. You can visit Favourite Florish at Mammy Market, Sapele Road, Warri during opening hours.
